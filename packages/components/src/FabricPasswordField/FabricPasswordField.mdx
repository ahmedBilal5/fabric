# FabricPasswordField

A password box with a show/hide button and an optional strength meter.

**Use it for** sign-in, sign-up and change-password forms. **Don't** show the strength meter on sign-in: it's only useful while choosing a password.

```tsx
<FabricPasswordField label="New password" value={password} onChange={(e) => setPassword(e.target.value)} strength />
```

## Strength meter

`strength` adds a four-step meter and one tip at a time ("Use at least 12 characters."). It also sets `autoComplete="new-password"`, so password managers offer to generate one.

<Canvas of={Stories.WithStrength} />
<Canvas of={Stories.StrengthLevels} />

The built-in `scorePassword` is a quick, dependency-free estimate: it rewards length and variety, and marks down common passwords, repeats and runs like "abcd". It isn't a security check. Pass `getStrength` to use your own, such as zxcvbn or a breached-password API, and enforce your real rules on the server.

## Accessibility

- The toggle is a real button named "Show password" / "Hide password", with `aria-pressed`.
- The meter's rating and tip are in the helper text, linked to the input and announced politely as they change. The colored bars are hidden from screen readers.
- `autoComplete` defaults to `current-password`, or `new-password` with `strength`.
- Spell-check and auto-capitalize are off.

## Relation to MUI

Built on `FabricTextField` (MUI `TextField`); every `TextField` prop works.
