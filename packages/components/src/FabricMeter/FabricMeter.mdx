# FabricMeter

A thin bar with a label and the value in words, for how much of something is used ("8 of 10 seats") or done ("3 of 5 steps").

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

<Controls of={Stories.Default} />

```tsx
<FabricMeter label="Seats" value={8} max={10} unit="seats" />
<FabricMeter kind="progress" label="Account setup" value={3} max={5} unit="steps" />
```

## When to use it

- **Quotas and limits** (the default `kind="quota"`): seats, storage, API calls, budget.
- **Progress** (`kind="progress"`): steps of a setup checklist, an import.
- For a key number with its change over time, use a stat card from `@fabric-kit/charts`. For a spinner or an unknown wait, use `FabricCircularProgress`.

## Quota levels

A quota turns amber at 80% and red at the limit, and says "Nearly full", "Limit reached" or "Over limit" beside an icon, so color is never the only cue. The value may go over `max`: the bar stays full, the text reads "12 of 10 seats" and the status says "Over limit". Change the points with `thresholds` (fractions of `max`) and the words with `statusText`.

<Canvas of={Stories.QuotaLevels} />

## Formats

`format` reads the value as `"fraction"` ("8 of 10 seats"), `"percent"` ("80%") or `"value"` ("8 seats"). For units such as gigabytes or money, pass the exact words in `valueText`.

## Progress

Progress is always the brand color and has no levels.

## In tables

`size="small"` draws a 4px bar; `hideLabel` keeps the label for screen readers only.

<Canvas of={Stories.InATable} />

## Accessibility

- A quota has `role="meter"`; progress has `role="progressbar"`. Both are named by the label and carry `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin`, `aria-valuemax` and an `aria-valuetext` that reads like the visible text, plus the status ("9 of 10 seats, Nearly full").
- `helperText` is linked as the description.
- The fill animates only when the user hasn't asked for reduced motion.

<Canvas of={Stories.ScreenReader} />

## Relation to MUI

MUI's `LinearProgress` is for loading. `FabricMeter` adds the label, the value in words, quota levels and the `meter` role, so it's its own component rather than a wrapper.
