# Stat card

`FabricStatCard` shows one key number with its change versus the previous period and, optionally, a sparkline of the trend. Lead a dashboard with a row of them.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

```tsx
<FabricStatCard
  label="Monthly recurring revenue"
  value={62_200}
  previousValue={58_900}
  comparisonLabel="vs last month"
  valueFormat={{ style: "currency", currency: "USD", maximumFractionDigits: 0 }}
  trend={mrrHistory}
/>
```

## Good news and bad news

The change is green when it's good news and red when it's bad. Most numbers should go up; for churn, wait times or errors set `goodDirection="down"`. Use `"neutral"` when neither way is better: the change shows in gray.

<Canvas of={Stories.Row4} />

## The change

Pass `previousValue` and the card works out the change, or pass `change` as a ratio (0.12 is +12%) when your API gives it. It's shown as a percentage, or with `changeFormat="absolute"` as an amount in the value's format (useful for rates, where "down 0.1%" means percentage points).

## Accessibility

- The change is never color alone: an arrow and the words "Up", "Down" or "No change" go with it. Screen readers hear "Down 5.3%" rather than the arrow.
- The card is a group named by its label. A clickable card is a link or button named by its label, value and change, in the order they appear, then the direction: "New customers 92 19.5% vs last month, up". Voice-control users can say what they see.
- The value uses proportional figures, since it stands alone; the sparkline has its own spoken summary.
