# Dashboard page

`FabricDashboardPage` is a page of key numbers and charts: a page header, one filter row that scopes everything below it, and a responsive grid of widgets. Fill it with [stat cards and chart cards](?path=/docs/charts-introduction--overview) from `@fabric-kit/charts`.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

```tsx
<FabricDashboardPage
  title="Sales overview"
  dateRange={range}
  onDateRangeChange={setRange}
  filters={<FabricSelect label="Region" options={regions} value={region} onChange={…} />}
  lastUpdated={data.fetchedAt}
  onRefresh={refetch}
  refreshing={isFetching}
>
  <FabricDashboardGrid>
    <FabricDashboardItem size="third">
      <FabricStatCard label="Revenue" value={data.revenue} previousValue={data.previousRevenue} />
    </FabricDashboardItem>
    <FabricDashboardItem size="twoThirds">
      <FabricChartCard title="Revenue">
        <FabricAreaChart loading={isFetching} xAxis={[{ data: days }]} series={[{ label: "Revenue", data: revenue }]} />
      </FabricChartCard>
    </FabricDashboardItem>
  </FabricDashboardGrid>
</FabricDashboardPage>
```

The page never fetches. It gives you the date range and filters; you load the data and pass it to the widgets.

## Filters

There is one filter row, above all the widgets, and every widget shows the same slice of data. Don't put filters inside individual cards: readers assume every number on the page covers the same period.

- `dateRange` adds `FabricDateRangePicker` with quick ranges (Last 7 days, This quarter, …). Turn the presets off with `datePresets={false}`, or pass your own list.
- `filters` adds your own controls to the same row.
- `lastUpdated` shows "Updated 4 minutes ago"; `onRefresh` adds a refresh button, disabled while `refreshing`.

## Layout

`FabricDashboardGrid` is a 12-column grid. Give each `FabricDashboardItem` a `size`:

| `size` | Phones | Tablets | Desktops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `quarter` | full | half | quarter |
| `third` | full | half | third |
| `half` | full | half | half |
| `twoThirds` | full | full | two thirds |
| `full` | full | full | full |

For anything else pass `span`, columns out of 12 per breakpoint, e.g. `span={{ xs: 12, md: 4 }}`. `rowSpan` makes a widget as tall as two rows beside it. Widgets in a row stretch to the same height.

Lead with a row of stat cards; put the chart that matters most next, at two thirds or full width.

## Loading

Pass `loading` to each widget. On the first load, stat cards show placeholders and charts say "Loading data…". On refresh, charts keep the last data on screen, dimmed, so the page doesn't jump.

## Accessibility

- The filter row is a `role="search"` region named "Dashboard filters".
- "Updated …" is announced politely when it changes.
- The page title is the `h1`. Give chart cards `headingLevel="h2"` so headings don't skip a level.
- Each widget brings its own semantics: stat cards are named groups, charts are named figures with a data table.

## Built from

`FabricPageHeader` (layout), `FabricDateRangePicker` (date-picker), `FabricIconButton` and `FabricRelativeTime` (components). Widgets come from `@fabric-kit/charts` and `FabricMeter`.
