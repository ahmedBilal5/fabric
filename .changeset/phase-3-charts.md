New package: `FabricBarChart`, `FabricLineChart`, `FabricAreaChart`, `FabricPieChart` and `FabricSparkline` on the free MUI X Charts, plus `FabricChartCard` (Chart/Table switch) and `FabricStatCard` (value, change versus last period, trend). Colors follow the theme, brand and dark mode, and every chart has a table of its data for screen readers.
