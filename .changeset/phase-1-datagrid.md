- `FabricBulkActionBar`: the bar that appears when rows are selected, with a count, actions, "Select all N", a "More actions" menu and Escape to clear. Inline or floating. `getSelectedCount` and `emptySelection` help with MUI X selection models.
- `FabricSavedViews` and `useSavedViews`: built-in and user-saved grid views with save, save as, rename, delete and an "Edited" state, kept in memory, in `localStorage`, or on your server.
